The method

Treat your life like a domain you take seriously — because you already know how to do that.

You wouldn't run a portfolio, a service line, or a surgical schedule without instrumentation — without knowing which numbers move, on what cadence, and why. Somewhere along the way, most of our clients stopped applying that discipline to anything outside of work. Vantage applies it to the rest. Sleep, attention, the state of your closest relationships, the goals you've quietly shelved — measured plainly, reviewed on a fixed schedule, adjusted on purpose instead of by accident.

Each engagement isolates one variable at a time. Not because your life has only one problem, but because changing three things at once is how competent people fail to change anything — you can't attribute the result, so you can't repeat it. We pick the variable with the highest leverage, set a baseline, run the change for a full quarter, and read the outcome before touching the next one.

None of this makes it clinical in the way that matters. The instrumentation is in service of a life, not a substitute for one — the numbers exist so the conversation can go somewhere honest instead of circling. Most clients say the review sessions are the most direct hour of their week.
